ПроНейронки Publisher

A small, single-operator tool that publishes one creator's own video content to their own connected accounts.

ПроНейронки Publisher is not a public product — it's automation built and run by one person to move finished videos from their own production pipeline onto the platforms they publish to, including TikTok, using each platform's official publishing API.

What it does:

  • Uploads a finished video (already produced by the operator) to their own connected TikTok account.
  • Sets the title, description, and other metadata the operator writes for that video.
  • Does not interact with, follow, message, or collect data about any other account.
